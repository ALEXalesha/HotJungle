## Unreleased

- 2026-10-26 - Published as a separate repository on GitHub.
- 2026-10-16 - "How to play" names the keys assigned right now, not the default ones.
- 2026-09-27 - Hard difficulty is fair: at most two flying enemies and one tankette at once; enemies and bullets live inside the same frame, nobody shoots from beyond the edge; a mine that blows up by itself gives no points and no trophy.
- 2026-09-27 - The left mouse button fires at the cursor, a mine can be shot while lying down, all game pixels are the same size (whole-number scale), the campaign counts only when all missions are done, broken saves no longer crash the game.
- 2026-09-27 - Pixel font JunglePixel drawn in code (Cyrillic and Latin) for the menu, windows and labels; the mission banner never covers the Pause window.
- 2026-09-26 - Campaign of 5 missions (forest fort, river at sunset, caves, secret base, tower assault) with different enemies, bosses, 2 checkpoints per mission and a victory screen; pits are always narrower than a jump; capsules with bonuses (spread, rapid fire, laser, shield); achievements pop up in the bottom right corner.
- 2026-09-26 - Fire with the left mouse button: a click shoots, holding it gives a burst at the same rate as J/X.
- 2026-09-26 - First version here, renamed to "Hot Jungle" as a fan version: fits into the window, physics independent of the screen refresh rate, start and pause, a pit costs a life even while blinking, no enemies in the water, record and sound, Russian text, sharp 8 px Cyrillic.

- 2026-09-26 - Огонь левой кнопкой мыши: клик стреляет, зажатая кнопка даёт очередь в том же темпе, что J/X.
